Why Dryer Vent Cleaning Matters

A clogged dryer vent can restrict airflow, cause your dryer to run longer, increase heat buildup, and create a serious fire concern. If your clothes are taking longer to dry, your dryer feels hot, or you notice a burning smell, it is time to have the vent checked.

Why Clean Your Dryer Vent?

The benefits go beyond fire prevention

Reduce Fire Risk

Lint buildup is highly flammable. Cleaning the vent helps reduce heat buildup and improves dryer safety.

Improve Efficiency

A clogged vent makes your dryer work harder and run longer. A clean vent helps restore better airflow.

Help Protect the Dryer

When your dryer overheats due to poor ventilation, it can wear out faster and work harder than necessary.

Faster Drying

If clothes are taking two or three cycles to dry, restricted airflow may be part of the problem.

Signs Your Dryer Vent Needs Cleaning

If you notice any of these, give us a call

Clothes Take Longer to Dry

If your dryer needs multiple cycles to dry a normal load, restricted airflow from lint buildup may be the cause.

Dryer Feels Hot

The outside of your dryer or your clothes may feel unusually hot after a cycle when the heat is not exhausting properly.

Burning Smell

A burning odor when the dryer runs is a serious warning sign. Stop using the dryer and schedule a vent inspection.

Weak Exhaust Flow

Check your exterior vent hood while the dryer runs. Weak airflow may indicate a clogged or restricted vent.

It's Been Over a Year

Many households benefit from annual dryer vent cleaning. High-use households may need service more often.

Long Vent Run

If your dryer is far from an exterior wall or the vent has multiple turns, lint can accumulate faster.
