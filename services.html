Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about gutter, dryer vent, and drainage services

How often should I have my gutters cleaned?

Most homes should have gutters cleaned at least twice a year, once in the spring and once in the fall after the leaves have fallen. If you have pine trees or heavy debris near your home, you may need more frequent cleanings.

What are the signs that I need gutter repairs?

Common signs include sagging gutters, gutters pulling away from the house, water marks on siding, water pooling around your foundation, visible rust or cracks, and leaks from joints or corners during rainfall.

Do gutter guards really work?

Yes, quality gutter guards work effectively when properly installed. While no system eliminates every future maintenance need, gutter guards can significantly reduce clogs and cleaning frequency.

Do you help with drainage and downspout extensions?

Yes. CleanFlow of Ohio helps with underground downspout extensions, roof runoff management, pooling water concerns, and drainage solutions designed to move water away from the home and foundation.

What areas do you serve?

CleanFlow of Ohio proudly serves the greater Miami Valley area. If you're unsure if we service your specific neighborhood, please give me a call.
