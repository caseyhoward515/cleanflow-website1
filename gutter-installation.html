The Danger of Failing Gutters:

When gutters leak or sag, water doesn't just fall — it pools at your foundation. This leads to basement seepage, foundation cracks, and hydrostatic pressure that can compromise your entire home's structure.

A seamless install today prevents a $10,000+ foundation repair tomorrow.

Signs You Need a Full Replacement

When cleaning and patching are no longer enough

Visible Leaks at Joints

Water dripping from seams or joints during rainfall means the connections have failed and sealant can only do so much.

Sagging or Pulling Away

Gutters separating from the fascia board indicate either structural failure, rotted fascia, or improper original installation.

Rust, Holes, and Corrosion

Corroded metal or holes in the gutter bottom mean the system has reached end-of-life and repairs won't hold.

Foundation Cracks or Staining

Cracks in basement walls or water stains on your foundation often trace back to a gutter system that's not directing water away properly.

Multiple Patched Sections

If your gutters have been repaired multiple times in different spots, the system is failing as a whole and it's time for a full replacement.

Faded or Peeling Finish

When the factory finish is gone, the bare metal is exposed to corrosion. New gutters with baked-on enamel will look better and last longer.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about gutter installation

How long do seamless gutters last?

Quality seamless aluminum gutters typically last 20-30 years with proper maintenance. Because there are no seams to separate or leak, they significantly outlast sectional systems that rely on joints and sealant.

What size gutters should I get?

Most residential homes in the Dayton area use 5-inch K-style gutters with 2x3 or 3x4 downspouts. Homes with steep roofs or large roof areas may benefit from 6-inch gutters for higher water capacity. I'll assess your specific roof during the estimate and recommend the right size.

Do you remove the old gutters?

Yes. Complete removal and disposal of your old gutter system is included in every installation. We also inspect and repair any fascia board damage before mounting the new system — this is a step many installers skip.

Can I add gutter guards at the same time?

Absolutely. Installing gutter guards at the same time as new gutters is the most cost-effective approach since we're already on the ladder. We offer quality guard systems with a 3-year guarantee. Learn more about our gutter guards.
