Signs You Need Gutter Cleaning

Click on each sign to learn more

If you notice water spilling over the sides of your gutters during rainfall, they're likely clogged with debris and need cleaning to prevent water damage.

Seeds carried by wind or birds can settle in debris-filled gutters and start growing. If you see greenery sprouting from your gutters, it's time for a cleaning.

Water stains on siding or foundation walls can be a sign that water isn't being properly moved away from your home.

Sagging gutters often mean the system is weighed down with debris and water. That extra weight can eventually cause sections to pull loose.

Standing water and decomposing debris in gutters can attract mosquitoes, carpenter ants, rodents, and other pests.

In winter, clogged gutters can contribute to ice dams and water backing up under shingles, which can lead to interior water damage.

Seasonal Gutter Maintenance Guide

Keep your gutters functioning properly year-round

Spring Gutter Maintenance

Spring is a critical time for gutter cleaning as trees shed flowers, seeds, and pollen that can quickly clog your gutter system.

  • Remove winter debris and accumulated leaves
  • Check for and repair any winter damage
  • Ensure downspouts are clear and directing water away from foundation
  • Inspect gutters for proper pitch and alignment
  • Consider gutter guard installation before heavy spring rains
Schedule Spring Cleaning

Summer Gutter Maintenance

Summer is the perfect time to inspect your gutters, clean out storm debris, and evaluate drainage concerns while weather conditions are favorable.

  • Check for nests from birds, wasps, or other pests
  • Inspect for sagging sections that need reinforcement
  • Clear debris from summer storms
  • Look for signs of rust or corrosion on metal gutters
  • Watch for pooling water around downspouts and foundation areas
Schedule Summer Maintenance

Fall Gutter Maintenance

Fall is the most important season for gutter maintenance as falling leaves and debris can quickly overwhelm your gutter system.

  • Clean gutters after the majority of leaves have fallen
  • Consider a second cleaning if you have many trees
  • Check downspouts for clogs and proper drainage
  • Inspect for loose gutter sections before winter
  • Consider installing gutter guards for long-term protection
Schedule Fall Cleaning

Winter Gutter Maintenance

Winter presents unique challenges for gutter systems, including ice dams and potential damage from snow and ice weight.

  • Monitor for ice dam formation during freeze and thaw cycles
  • Check gutters after heavy snow or ice storms
  • Look for signs of gutters pulling away due to ice weight
  • Ensure downspouts are clear for melting snow drainage
  • Plan for a comprehensive spring cleaning
Schedule Winter Inspection

Benefits of Professional Gutter Cleaning

Why proper gutter maintenance is essential for your home

Protect Your Foundation

Properly functioning gutters direct water away from your home's foundation, helping prevent cracks, leaks, and structural damage.

Preserve Exterior

Clean gutters prevent overflow that can damage siding, fascia boards, soffits, and painted surfaces.

Protect Landscaping

Properly diverted rainwater protects garden beds, plants, mulch, and soil around your property.

Prevent Pests

Clean gutters help eliminate standing water and debris that attract mosquitoes, termites, rodents, and other pests.
