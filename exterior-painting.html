Signs Your Home Needs Fresh Paint

If you notice any of these, your home's protection is failing

Peeling or Flaking

Paint curling away from the surface means the bond has failed and moisture is entering the wood beneath.

Severe Fading

UV rays break down paint pigments over time. Faded paint loses its protective seal, leaving your home exposed.

Cracking and Chalking

A powdery residue when you touch the surface means the paint is oxidizing and no longer protecting the substrate.

Visible Wood Rot

Soft spots or holes in your trim indicate that paint failed long ago and water has already begun rotting the structure.

Outdated Colors

Beyond protection, fresh paint dramatically increases curb appeal and home value. A good paint job pays for itself at resale.

It's Been 7+ Years

Even if paint looks okay from the ground, 7-10 years is the typical lifespan for exterior paint in Ohio's climate.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about exterior painting

How long does an exterior paint job last in Ohio?

With proper surface preparation and premium paints, a professional exterior paint job in Ohio typically lasts 7-10 years. Our freeze-thaw cycles and humidity make prep work especially critical — that's why we never skip steps.

What time of year is best for exterior painting?

The ideal window is late spring through early fall, when temperatures are consistently above 50°F and humidity is moderate. We typically paint from April through October. Schedule early in the season for the best availability.

Do I need to be home during the painting?

No. We document all work with before and after photos and communicate progress throughout the project. Many of our customers are at work while we paint. We treat every property with the same respect whether you're watching or not.

What kind of paint do you use?

We use premium-grade exterior acrylic latex paints from trusted manufacturers. These paints are specifically formulated for UV resistance, flexibility in temperature swings, and long-term adhesion. We're happy to discuss specific brands and color options during your estimate.
